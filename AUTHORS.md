# Authors & Contributors

**ATARI Research Lab** (Advanced Technologies for Ambient-Assisted Living and Research on
Innovation), part of the University of Cádiz, Spain.

## CRediT (Contributor Roles Taxonomy)

This project follows the [CRediT](https://credit.niso.org/) taxonomy for contributor roles:

### Conceptualization

**Daniel Sanchez-Morillo**, Diego Sales-Lerida

Ideas; formulation or evolution of overarching research goals and aims.

### Hardware Design

**Diego Sales-Lerida**, **Alejandro Lara-Doña**

PCB design, electronic circuit development, component selection and hardware integration.

### Enclosure Design

**Patricia Camacho-Magriñán**, Diego Sales-Lerida, Alejandro Lara-Doña

Design and prototyping of device enclosure using CAD software (SOLIDWORKS/Fusion360), 3D printing
and mechanical integration.

### Firmware Development

**Diego Sales-Lerida**, **Alejandro Lara-Doña**

Embedded software architecture, implementation, optimization and debugging.

### Software

**Patricia Camacho-Magriñán**, Daniel Sánchez-Morillo, Diego Sales-Lerida

Programming, software development, algorithm design and implementation of supporting tools.

### Device Testing

Diego Sales-Lerida, Alejandro Lara-Doña, Patricia Camacho-Magriñán

Comprehensive testing of assembled devices, quality assurance and validation of final units before deployment.

### Documentation

**Alejandro Lara-Doña**, Diego Sales-Lerida

Creation and maintenance of technical documentation, user guides and API reference.

### Validation

All authors contributed to verification of system functionality, data quality and reproducibility
of results.

### Investigation

**Daniel Sanchez-Morillo**, Patricia Camacho-Magriñán, Diego Sales-Lerida

Research activities, literature review and experimental design for clinical studies.

### Data Curation

**Patricia Camacho-Magriñán**, **Diego Sales-Lerida**, Daniel Sanchez-Morillo

Management, annotation and maintenance of research data for reproducibility.

### Visualization

**Patricia Camacho-Magriñán**, Diego Sales-Lerida

Preparation and presentation of data visualizations, figures and graphical representations.

### Supervision

**Daniel Sanchez-Morillo**

Oversight and leadership for research activity planning and execution.

### Project Administration

**Daniel Sanchez-Morillo**

Management and coordination of research planning and execution.

### Funding Acquisition

**Daniel Sanchez-Morillo**

Acquisition of financial support for the research project.
